# AcademicApply 1.8.0

Completed, failed and cancelled tasks now retain a structured summary in Activity. A completion card appears automatically in the current screen with a link to the full summary. Search summaries include distinct listings, new and updated records, candidate links, strong matches, closing deadlines and source access outcomes. Document tasks show package documents, supporting files, remaining requirements and PDF exports where applicable. Existing task logs remain available; older source warnings are translated into actionable access reports without changing the saved logs.

Search repairs distinguish HTTP 401/403 or verification pages, published crawl restrictions, manual sources, employer configuration and temporary network/server errors. Temporary requests retry once; long Retry-After windows are deferred. Repeated blocked sources pause public checks for 15 minutes, temporary failures for at least one minute. Test source / Test connector explicitly rechecks access. Published restrictions and human verification remain in effect. Provider recovery searches for public official employer alternatives when AI processing and provider web research are enabled.

Unreadable detail pages now enter recovery even when their source index was readable. Pagination failures retain the first page. Detail review has a separate bounded budget, ranked by selected roles and keywords. Recovery runs at most three batches of six source hints, rotating deferred hints on saved runs. Source hints do not establish that every hinted portal was read.

Short or invalid evidence entries no longer reject otherwise valid candidates. Candidates still require at least one valid evidence excerpt, public validated source URLs, and eligibility for the selected filters. Unsafe candidate URLs are excluded before any fetch. New/update totals count distinct records across sources, provider recovery and MCP results. Failed scheduled searches advance to their next scheduled occurrence instead of immediately retrying every minute. One-off searches and document tasks have their missing Activity result/retry actions restored.

The 18 November 2026 source check returned candidate links from 19 of 103 catalog entries; 54 needed browser assistance or returned no extractable links, 13 had temporary failures, 9 published restrictions, 7 needed employer configuration, and one was manual. Greenhouse and Ashby sample boards returned 26 and 78 records respectively; the tested Lever board returned 404. These are dated access checks, not a guarantee of market coverage or a claim that blocked portals can be scraped.

Validation: 99 automated tests, the completion-summary Electron workflow, flexible role/keyword regression, and packaged interface checks. A real Codex recovery run returned one employer vacancy with two evidence sources after the original portal was simulated as HTTP 403. Its evidence remains marked AI-reported pending official review. No real application or email was submitted.
